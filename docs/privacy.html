Privacy Policy

Auto Organizer for Mac, by May Morgan · Effective October 27, 2026

Auto Organizer does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

What the app accesses

  • Auto Organizer runs in Apple's App Sandbox. It can only reach the folder you choose in the standard macOS folder picker.
  • It reads the names of the files directly inside that folder and moves them into category folders inside the same folder. It never opens or reads the contents of your files, and it does not look inside subfolders or anywhere else on your Mac.

What the app stores

  • To make Undo possible, the app saves a small hidden file named .autoorganizer-last-run.tsv inside the folder you organized. It lists each moved file's original and new location.
  • This file stays on your Mac. It is deleted when you use Undo Last Run, and replaced the next time you organize that folder. You can also delete it yourself at any time.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet and never sends any information off your Mac.
  • It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party services.
  • It does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
